About Orin

Our
Story

Orin didn't start as an agency with a slide deck and a mission statement. It started with developers and designers who kept getting hired to fix half-finished projects from "cheaper" vendors. Somewhere between the third emergency WordPress fix and the fifth "why isn't my site on Google" call, we realized businesses didn't need another vendor — they needed a team that understood code, design, and growth as one connected thing, not three separate invoices.

So we built that team. What started as a small group taking on side projects turned into a full studio, handling everything from custom web applications to full-funnel marketing campaigns — always with the same rule: if it doesn't help the client's business, we don't ship it just to look busy.

Today, Orin works with founders launching their first product, established brands rebuilding what no longer fits, and marketing teams who need a technical partner that actually understands what they're trying to achieve.

How We Work
Our Values

  • Strategy First

    We don't open a code editor or a design file until we know what "success" looks like for you, specifically — not in general.

  • Built to Scale

    Whatever we build — a Shopify store, a React app, a design system — it's built to grow with your business, not to be rebuilt from scratch in a year.

  • No Black Boxes

    You'll always know what we're doing, why we're doing it, and what it's costing you. No jargon used to avoid answering a simple question.

  • Data Over Guesswork.

    From SEO to performance marketing, we let numbers make the case, not opinions or industry buzzwords.

  • Ownership, Not Just Delivery

    We treat every project like our own reputation is riding on it — becauseit is.
